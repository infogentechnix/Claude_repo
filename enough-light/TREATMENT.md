# Enough Light — Treatment

**Style:** Watercolor Brush (`styles/watercolor`) · **Length:** 30.0 s · **Format:** 1920×1080, 60 fps (the style's rate) · **Voice:** warm female English narrator (Kokoro), subtitles burned in plus `.srt` · **Loudness:** −14 LUFS

Topic from the brief: *the power of a prayer, which can make you happy at the end.* No faith is named, so the prayer stays universal: folded hands, closed eyes, no symbols of any one religion.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The handwritten worry, answered by painting.** A worried line is written on blank paper, and the prayer is painted as an answer: the ink of the worry turns into flowers. Strong for this style (a journal page), but abstract, with no person to feel for.
2. **One figure, before and after.** A woman sits on a step in the rain, head bowed. She folds her hands and prays; a warm wash spreads from her hands across the whole sheet; the rain stays, but she lifts her face and smiles. A clear subject, one turn, and the palette itself carries the change.
3. **A paper lantern from night to dawn.** A prayer lantern rises through a blue-grey night into a peach sunrise. Pretty, but it is about the lantern, not about how the person feels, and lanterns tie it to particular traditions.

**Chosen: 2, opened and closed with the hand-written line of 1.** The person gives the feeling a face, and the warm wash spreading from her folded hands is the one moment only watercolour can do: transparent pigment blooming over the wet sheet, re-colouring what was already painted. The handwritten bookend lets the ending echo the opening exactly.

## 2. Logline and arc

*On a grey day, a woman too tired to go on stops to pray, and a small warm light spreads from her hands until the whole page, and her face, is full of sun.*

- **Setup (0–11 s):** a heavy line on blank paper; rain; a woman painted in alone on a step, head bowed.
- **Turn (11–23 s):** her hands fold; a single warm point of gold blooms between them and spreads across the sheet; the cold blue-grey turns to peach and gold; the raindrops on the page become small blossoms.
- **Ending (23–30 s):** she stands, face lifted, smiling; the opening line is washed out by mist and a new one writes itself in its place. The brush lifts before the last stroke is finished.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Naturalist field-journal watercolours and watercolour picture books in general** (the grammar, not any one book).
  - *Learn:* generous paper margins, a single subject per page, colour temperature as emotion, handwriting as a quiet second voice.
  - *Don't take:* any character design, page layout, illustrated scene or lettering from a specific book.
- **Slow, single-take emotional shorts built on one colour change** (the grammar of "the world warms up around one person").
  - *Learn:* hold on the face; let one change happen slowly and fully; music that starts thin and only blooms at the turn.
  - *Don't take:* shots, melodies or characters.

## 8. Subtitles and titles

- Subtitles: Cormorant Garamond italic, about 36 px, centred at the bottom, ink at 78 % over a paper-coloured halo; at most two lines; held at least max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s).
- Handwritten lines: Caveat, drawn twice over a paper halo, revealed left to right like writing. *"Too heavy today."* in blue-grey ink; *"Lighter now."* in warm sepia with the gold accent on the full stop.
- No title card, no end card, no credit line in the picture.

## 9. Palette

- Cold (opening): paper `#f2ead9`, rain sky `#a9b6bf`, street `#7d8a93`, coat `#5d6f80`, ink `#3a3530`.
- Warm (ending): sky `#f3d9b0`, light `#efc27a`, street `#c9a27e`, coat `#8a6a5a`, blossoms `#e89a8c`.
- **Accent:** gold `#e0a53a`, reserved for the light between her hands (it becomes the sun, and the full stop of the last line).
- Every painted element blends cold → warm with the spread of the wash, in 5 bands.
